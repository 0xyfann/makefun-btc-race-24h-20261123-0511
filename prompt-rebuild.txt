Strategy: "Tranched trend rider with a disaster brake"
- ENTER: buy the full $1,000 starting capital in 8 equal tranches of
  cash//8 ($125 each), one per minute over the first 8 batches.
- HOLD: no rebalancing, no noise trading.
- BRAKE: sell everything if close < 480-bar (8h) SMA AND close is at
  least 200 bps below the 480-bar rolling peak.
- RE-ENTRY: after a brake, stay flat until the close is above the
  120-bar (2h) SMA for 30 consecutive minutes, then rebuild in 8 tranches.
- All decisions in exact integer arithmetic; no clock, no env, no random.
  max_slippage_bps 10000 (wide open, like the house baselines).

Why this design (dev-window evidence, 1,440 one-minute bars):
- The dev window grinds up +3.64% (first close $58,956.59, last
  $61,102.18). Buy-and-hold makes +$32.17, 1 trade, max DD 1.98%.
- Every active 1-minute strategy LOSES to the ~46 bps full-size round
  trip (4 bps fee + 2 bps slippage + ~17 bps impact per leg at freeze
  depth): EMA cross 5/13 -$146.94, 8/21 -$120.09, 21/50 -$34.10;
  Donchian 48 -$3.48; momentum lb24 th80 -$11.75; RSI/Bollinger -$44
  to -$103. 1-minute noise (std 8.12 bps) swamps any signal; only
  multi-hour trends are tradeable, and this window has no tradeable
  pullback bigger than the round-trip cost.
- Splitting the entry across 8 bars cuts impact roughly 8x (impact is
  quadratic in size per bar, and each bar's pool is fresh): the rider
  beats buy-and-hold by +$3.19 on dev.
- Slow trend filters were tested too (EMA 30/120 +$4.40, SMA 60 +$20.48)
  but all underperform buy-and-hold on this window and lose on rolling
  12h/6h sub-windows; buy-and-hold had the best mean (+$8.85 per 12h
  window) and win rate (5/7) of anything tested.
- The brake is set at 200 bps because 150 bps whipsaws (19 trades on
  dev, +$12.47) and 250-300 bps exits too late on a falling market.
  It never fires on dev, so the bot keeps the full trend gain.

Backtest numbers (exact integer fill math replicated from the harness
engine sim/bars/engine.ts; verified against the real bot over the wire):
- Dev window: net PnL +$35.36, 8 trades (fills), max drawdown 1.98%,
  fees $0.81. Passes the dev gate (PnL > 0) and beats the buy-and-hold
  baseline (+$32.17), which the prize requires.
- Reversed dev window (falling-market stress test): -$19.95, 9 trades,
  max DD 2.69% — vs buy-and-hold -$38.39. The brake works.
- V-shaped window (fall then recovery): -$5.20, 17 trades, max DD
  2.96% — brake fires on the fall, re-entry rebuilds on the recovery.
- Determinism: two full protocol replays produce byte-identical replies.
